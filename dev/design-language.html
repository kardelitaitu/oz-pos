Anti-Aliasing

Always render text at the highest quality. The body applies subpixel antialiasing on all platforms for crisp, consistent text.

Aa
✓ Antialiased
Smooth, consistent rendering
Aa
✗ Auto (default)
Platform-dependent, inconsistent

Applied on body: -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; -moz-osx-font-smoothing: grayscale;

This ensures Inter and JetBrains Mono render with consistent weight and clarity across Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android — no bold/thin surprises between platforms.
