## Stock levels

Stock is tracked per product per warehouse. Sales decrement stock
automatically, and low-stock alerts flag products below their threshold.

## Adjustments

Stock adjustments record a reason and a movement ledger entry, so any change
can be traced back to who did it and when.

## Warehouses

A business can have multiple warehouses and registers. Each register serves
from its assigned warehouse, and stock moves between warehouses are recorded
movements.
