## Store settings

Business name, currency, receipt layout, and hardware defaults are
configured here and synced to every register.

## Local data

All data is stored locally on each device and synced to the tenant's cloud
store. Backups of the local database can be exported for disaster recovery.

## Security

Monetary values are always stored as integer minor units; staff accounts are
PIN-based with role-based permissions, and every action is audited.
