## Add a product

Open Products, add a product with a name, SKU, price, and starting stock.
Prices are stored in minor units, so no floating-point rounding surprises.

## Ring up a sale

Tap the product (or scan a barcode), choose a quantity, and hit Pay. The sale
is committed locally and appears in the day's history instantly.

## What happens offline

The sale is queued locally and synced automatically once the device is back
online. Nothing is lost and nothing blocks the counter.
