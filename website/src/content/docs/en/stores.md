## The topology editor

Stores, branches, registers, and warehouses are arranged in a visual diagram.
Wires connect devices to their locations, and the editor validates the layout
as you go.

## Plan limits

The number of stores, registers, and warehouses is set by your plan tier.
The editor flags anything that exceeds your limits before you apply it.

## Keep devices in sync

Devices pull the topology when they reconnect, so a new register appears on
every screen without manual setup.
