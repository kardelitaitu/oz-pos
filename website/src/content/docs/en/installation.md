## System requirements

The same offline-first core runs on every platform:

- Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
- macOS 12 or newer
- Linux (x86_64, glibc 2.31+)

4 GB RAM and 1 GB of free disk space are recommended.

## Download and install

Download the installer for your platform from the releases page, then run it
and follow the setup wizard. No account is required — the 90-day free trial
starts on first launch.

## First launch

The first screen asks for your business name and default currency. You can
change both later in settings.
