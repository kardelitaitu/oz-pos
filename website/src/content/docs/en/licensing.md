## Plans

Plans are `trial`, `pro`, `premium`, and `enterprise`. The 90-day trial
(`trial`) unlocks the full product; paid plans add more stores, registers,
warehouses, cloud sync, and automation features.

## Expiry and grace

Subscriptions carry an expiry date and a grace period. When the subscription
expires, the app enters grace and keeps working offline until the grace date,
then degrades to the free tier.

## Machine limits

Each paid plan allows a number of activated registers. Machines are
hardware-bound and can be revoked by the tenant admin.
