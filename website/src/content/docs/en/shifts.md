## Opening a shift

A cashier opens a shift on a register, optionally recording a starting float.
Only that cashier's sales count toward the shift.

## Closing and reconciling

Closing a shift shows expected totals versus actual counts and flags
discrepancies before the register accepts the close.

## Audit history

Every sale, void, refund, and stock adjustment is recorded with the user and
terminal that made it, so shifts reconcile back to a complete audit trail.
